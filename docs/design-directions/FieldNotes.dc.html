Dual AGN, binary black holes, and the gravitational waves they will make.

Ph.D. candidate at Carnegie Mellon. I count massive black hole pairs across cosmic time in DESI and the ASTRID simulation, from kiloparsec dual AGN to sub-parsec binaries, to forecast what LISA and pulsar timing arrays will detect.

Research

One question at three separation scales: where are the pairs, how many are there, and which ones will gravitational-wave detectors hear?
01 / KPC
DESI DR1 · 2026

Cosmic Pairs: a DESI census of dual and offset AGN

More than 7,000 dual AGN, one to two orders of magnitude beyond earlier samples at z = 0.2–0.4, fifty dwarf duals, and a tripled census above z = 2.

arXiv Explorer Project page
02 / SUB-PC
DESI DR3 · in prep

Massive black hole binaries hiding in DESI spectra

Doppler shifts of broad emission lines betray sub-parsec binaries. Velocity-offset searches, then variational autoencoders on DR3, feeding LISA and PTA event-rate forecasts.

DESI-internal results Project page
03 / HOSTS
ASTRID · 2024

Dual AGN host galaxies are disky

Kinematic decomposition of about 1,000 dual AGN hosts at z = 2 in ASTRID: 61% are disk-dominated, not the disturbed remnants merger theory expects.
