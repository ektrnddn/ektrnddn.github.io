01 · kpc
DESI DR1 · 2026

Cosmic Pairs: a DESI census of dual and offset AGN

More than 7,000 dual AGN, one to two orders of magnitude beyond earlier samples at z = 0.2–0.4, fifty dwarf duals, and a tripled census above z = 2. ASTRID predicts 76% merge by z = 0.

arXiv:2607.27390 Interactive explorer
02 · sub-pc
DESI DR3 · in prep

Massive black hole binaries hiding in DESI spectra

Doppler shifts of broad emission lines betray sub-parsec binaries. Velocity-offset searches, then variational autoencoders on DR3, feeding LISA and PTA event-rate forecasts.

Results page (DESI members) Project page
03 · hosts
ASTRID · 2024

Dual AGN host galaxies are disky

Kinematic decomposition of about 1,000 dual AGN hosts at z = 2: 61% are disk-dominated, not the disturbed remnants merger theory expects. Mock images match HST dual quasar hosts.
