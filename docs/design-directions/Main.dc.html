Research

One question at three separation scales: where are the massive black hole pairs, how many are there, and which ones will gravitational-wave detectors hear?
01 · kpc scale
DESI DR1 · 2026

Cosmic Pairs: a DESI census of dual and offset AGN

More than 7,000 dual AGN from DESI DR1: one to two orders of magnitude beyond earlier samples at z = 0.2–0.4, about fifty dwarf dual AGN, and a tripled census above z = 2. Secondary hosts sit 0.3 dex above the star-forming main sequence, and ASTRID predicts 76% of these pairs merge by z = 0.

02 · sub-parsec
DESI DR3 · in preparation

Massive black hole binaries hiding in DESI spectra

Sub-parsec binaries betray themselves through Doppler shifts of their broad emission lines. I am searching DESI for these velocity offsets and scaling the search with variational autoencoders on DR3, so that candidate counts feed directly into LISA and PTA event-rate forecasts.

03 · host galaxies
ASTRID · z ≈ 2 · 2024

Dual AGN host galaxies are disky

Kinematic decomposition of about 1,000 dual AGN hosts at z = 2 in the ASTRID simulation: 61% are disk-dominated rather than the disturbed remnants merger theory expects, and mock images reproduce the Sérsic indices and sizes of HST dual quasar hosts.
